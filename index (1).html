Cross-Device Sync

Your bids and rates are stored locally on each device. Use Export and Import to move data between devices, or keep a running backup.

How to sync across devices
  1. On your main device, tap Export All Data — saves a .json file
  2. Email or AirDrop that file to your other device
  3. Open this app on the other device, tap Import Data
  4. All bids and rates will appear immediately
Add to Home Screen (iPhone / iPad)

This app works as a full-screen app on iOS — no App Store required.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add — it appears on your home screen like a real app
Hosting This App (Free)

To access from any device at a real URL, host on GitHub Pages for free:

  1. Go to github.com and create a free account
  2. Click New repository — name it kmt-estimator, make it Public
  3. Click uploading an existing file, drag and drop index.html
  4. Click Commit changes
  5. Go to Settings → Pages, set Source to main branch, click Save
  6. Your URL will be: https://yourusername.github.io/kmt-estimator
  7. Bookmark that URL on every device — it works on any browser
